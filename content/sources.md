# Paraguay Website — Sources

Companion to `paraguay-research.md`. Source IDs (`[S#]`) are cited there.

**Tier** — how much to trust it:
- **A** — Official, government, UNESCO, intergovernmental, or academic/peer-reviewed.
- **B** — Reputable reference work, conservation NGO, or established news outlet.
- **C** — Secondary or aggregator source (Wikipedia, travel blogs, park directories). Use only to back up a claim, and check it before quoting anything precise.

**Access note (Sep 2026):** The research environment could not open `travel.state.gov`, `migraciones.gov.py`, or `visitparaguay.travel` (blocked by the network proxy). Their content was seen only through search-result summaries, or not at all. Check them directly before publishing anything they cover.
